01 · Agriculture

Grains & pulses
from the Prairies.

Sourced directly from farmers in Alberta, Saskatchewan, and Manitoba. Graded for human consumption and certified to Canadian Grain Commission standards. Available graded or ungraded, in bulk or containerized.

Wheat & oats

Durum Wheat

High-gluten Prairie durum with a bright yellow hue. Used worldwide for premium pasta, couscous, and Mediterranean foods.

Pasta · Couscous

Hard Red Spring Wheat

High-protein wheat trusted by millers and bakers worldwide. Excellent milling and baking characteristics, ideal for bread flour blends.

Bread · Milling

CWRS Wheat

Canada Western Red Spring — the standard-bearer of Canadian premium milling wheat. Strong, consistent quality across grades.

Premium milling

Canadian Oats

Whole and rolled oats from Prairie farms. Used in cereals, snacks, granola, and animal feed. Available in food and feed grades.

Cereals · Snacks

Lentils

Whole Red Lentils

Vibrant crimson lentils with intact skins. Used in dals, soups, and stews across South Asia, the Middle East, and Africa.

Dal · Soup

Laird Lentils

Large green lentils grown primarily in Saskatchewan. Prized internationally for size and consistency.

Premium grade

Split Red Lentils

Whole red lentils split and dehulled. Quick cooking, ideal for industrial food processing and packaged ready-meals.

Quick-cook

Richlea Lentils

Medium-sized green lentils. A versatile commercial grade, used widely in soups, salads, and bulk pulse blends.

Medium green

Eston Lentils

Small green lentils with a delicate flavor. Common in Mediterranean and South American markets.

Small green

Peas

Whole Green Peas

Used in soups, flours, and traditional recipes worldwide. Nutrient-dense, protein-rich, and a stable food source.

Soup · Flour

Split Green Peas

Quick-cooking, mild flavor. Staple in global cuisines and a key ingredient in pea flour and protein blends.

Quick-cook

Split Yellow Peas

A staple of Indian, Middle Eastern, and Caribbean cuisines. Reliable supply with flexible packaging.

Dal · Snack

Whole Yellow Peas

Versatile commercial-grade pulse, used for animal feed, pea flour, and plant-protein extraction.

Feed · Protein

Marrowfat Peas

Large, starchy peas used for mushy peas, snack-pea products, and certain Asian markets where size matters.

Snack-grade

Chickpeas

Canadian Kabuli chickpeas. Used worldwide in hummus, snacks, salads, and traditional dishes.

Hummus · Snack

Beans

Black Beans

Uniform in color and flavor. Widely used across Latin American, Caribbean, and North American cuisines.

Soup · Rice

Cranberry Beans

Mottled pink beans with creamy texture. Popular in Italian and Spanish cuisines for stews, salads, and pasta dishes.

Italian · Spanish

Dark Red Kidney Beans

Firm-textured beans used in chili, red beans & rice, and Caribbean preparations. A workhorse of the bean category.

Chili · Rice

Great Northern Beans

Medium-sized white beans with a delicate flavor. Used in baked beans, soups, and casseroles.

Baked · Casserole

Baked beans

02 · Energy

Oil, gas & biofuels
from Western Canada.

Crude oil, condensate, propane, and refined products from Western Canadian basins. Biodiesel and renewable feedstocks for compliance- market buyers. Spot, term, and structured trades.

Western Canadian Crude

WCS and synthetic crude from Alberta oil-sands and conventional basins. Available pipeline, rail, and marine spec.

WCS · Synthetic

Condensate

Natural gas liquids used as diluent for heavy-crude blending and as petrochemical feedstock. Pipeline and rail delivery.

Diluent · Petrochem

Propane / LPG

Bulk propane and LPG from Western Canadian fractionators. Marine cargoes and rail movements to North American and Asian buyers.

Heating · Petrochem

Biodiesel (B100)

Tallow- and canola-based biodiesel meeting ASTM and EN spec. For RFS, LCFS, and other compliance-market obligations.

RFS · LCFS

Renewable Feedstocks

Used cooking oil, tallow, distillers corn oil, and canola — feedstocks for renewable-diesel and SAF producers.

UCO · Tallow · DCO

Refined Products

Gasoline, diesel, and jet-fuel cargoes from Canadian refineries. Spot trades and short-term term contracts.

Gasoline · Diesel · Jet
