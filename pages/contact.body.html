Common questions

Before you write.

Why do you require an LOI for pricing?

A firm price ties our hand on physical product we have to source, hedge, and ship. We won't put a number on the table unless there's a real trade behind it. The LOI is how we tell — it costs us nothing to issue an offer if the buyer is ready; it costs us a lot to issue one if they aren't.

What's the minimum cargo size for an LOI?

Bulk: 25,000 MT and up per shipment. Containers: minimum one 20-ft FCL (~20–25 MT). Below that, we point you to a regional aggregator — we're not the right counterparty.

Do we sign an NCND first?

A mutual NCND covers every LOI from the moment you submit. We don't show your trade around the market, you don't approach our producers directly. If you want a formal NCND/MFPA signed before submitting, request one with your inquiry.

Which payment instruments do you accept?

Irrevocable LC at sight or confirmed LC from a Tier-1 issuing bank is the standard. Deferred LC, TT, and CAD are acceptable case-by-case. Open account is reserved for counterparties we've transacted with before.

How do you verify a buyer?

We do a light KYC on every new counterparty: company registration, beneficial ownership, sanctions screening, and at least one trade reference. For the buyer it's a 10-minute exercise. For us it's how we sleep at night.

Will you reply if I'm not yet ready to LOI?

Yes — use the quick inquiry form. You'll get general availability, lead times, and capability info. You won't get a price. That's not gatekeeping; it's how a real trading desk works.

Do you handle documentation?

Yes — phytosanitary, dangerous-goods, certificate of origin, weight & quality, fumigation. Anything the buyer's bank or port authority needs, we prepare and file.

How fast will I see an offer after submitting the LOI?

One to two business days for standard contracts. Three to five for structured, multi-year, or non-standard trades. We confirm receipt the same day so you're never wondering.
