About

A Canadian commodity trader.

Mercatorum is a Canada-based merchant house. We buy agricultural products, energy, and chemicals direct from Canadian producers and sell them to buyers in more than forty countries. Our trading desk sits in Alberta, at the logistical heart of Western Canada's commodity economy.

What we do

Three product lines. One desk.

Mercatorum trades across three commodity classes — agriculture, energy, and chemicals. They share a common logic: bulk movement, producer relationships built over years, and a paperwork standard that holds up at any port in the world.

We're not a broker. We take the trade. That means we carry the quality, the documentation, and the risk that attaches to each cargo from origin to discharge.

We work with farmers in the Prairies, refiners and producers in the oil-sands region, and fertilizer and chemical plants across Western Canada. Our buyers sit in 40+ markets across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia.

Why Mercatorum

A focused desk.
Global reach.

The name is Latin — of merchants — and it describes the work plainly: connecting Canadian producers to buyers who need what they grow, refine, and produce.

"A senior desk of ten-plus traders and ops — lean enough that every cargo gets a name and a number, deep enough to move bulk vessels and multi-year offtake."

Canadian commodities are among the most reliable in the world. The country produces them at scale, certifies them with rigorous government inspection, and ships them through deep-water ports on both coasts. Our job is to be a clear, fair channel between that supply and the markets that need it.

What we hold to

Four standards.

01 · Direct sourcing

We buy from named producers under long-term relationships. No spot-market bidding. No anonymous resellers.

02 · Clean paperwork

Phytosanitary, dangerous-goods, certificate-of-origin, and weight-and-quality certificates handled by our team on every cargo.

03 · Flexible terms

FOB, CFR, CIF, and structured financing where it makes sense. Smaller cargoes accepted alongside bulk vessels.

04 · Discretion

What you trade is your business. We don't talk about buyers, sellers, or volumes outside the trade.

Headquarters

Edmonton ·
Alberta, Canada.

Edmonton is the gateway between the Canadian Prairies, the oil sands, and the country's two coasts. It's where the trains originate, the pipelines converge, and the fertilizer plants ship from. Setting up here means we're close to the work.

From Edmonton, our cargoes route through Vancouver and Prince Rupert for the Asia-Pacific basin, through Thunder Bay and the St. Lawrence for Europe, and overland into the United States.
